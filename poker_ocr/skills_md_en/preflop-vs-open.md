> Source: "Little Green Book" methodology §6, key preflop numbers (split from the original `coachMethodology`, body unmodified).

- Domination: AK vs AQ ≈ 74%; AK vs 72 only 67%; AK vs QJ only 65%.
  87s against AA/KK/AKs/AQs ≈ 32%, while AJ is only 25.7%.

- Strong hand, call or re-raise: in position lean toward calling to trap; in the blinds lean toward re-raising to erase the positional disadvantage.
  Almost always re-raise KK/QQ — KK fears an A on the flop (~17%), QQ fears an A or K (~35%).
