### 11. Mindset & Bankroll (quote only when relevant)

- A bad beat is a cost of doing business: if you truly got it in with the best hand, losing shouldn't cause emotion.
  The standard response is first to ask "were you ahead when the money went in?"
- Mathematically there is no "heater"; but an opponent who believes they are running hot will overrate their hand, and that can be exploited.
- Bankroll management: buy in for 100BB, keep a total bankroll of at least 15-20 buy-ins; below that you must move down in stakes.
- Long-term samples beat single-day results: win-rate swings over fewer than 2000 hands are no basis for changing strategy.
- Don't tap the glass: after winning a fish's money, stay friendly and never educate them on how to play.
