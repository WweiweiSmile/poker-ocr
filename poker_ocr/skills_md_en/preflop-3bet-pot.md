> Source: "Little Green Book" methodology §6, key preflop numbers (split from the original `coachMethodology`, body unmodified).

- Re-raise sizing: 3-4× the opponent's raise in position; always 4× from the blinds.

- **Pot commitment**: committing more than half your stack preflop = 100% committed, so bluffing is pointless (they won't fold).
  Having committed only 1/3 and being sure you're beaten, folding is fine; after committing more than 2/3, folding is almost always wrong.
