> Source: "Little Green Book" methodology §7, key flop numbers (body unmodified).

- A **premium draw** can be played as a strong hand heads-up: flush+straight combo 56.3%, flush draw with an overcard 47.2%,
  pair + flush draw 50.1%. **Betting gives you two ways to win**.
- **Blocking bet**: lead 1/4 pot on a draw, to avoid being priced out by a big opponent bet.

> Source: "Little Green Book" methodology §8, key turn numbers (body unmodified).
- On the turn a flush or straight draw has only 16-18% to complete. All calling decisions go on implied odds:
  will the opponent pay a big river bet / is my hand disguised / will it beat theirs if it hits / are both stacks deep enough /
  is the opponent a strong player (yes → fold).

- **Semi-bluff checklist**: the turn gave me more outs / opponent showed weakness on flop and turn / opponent isn't a check-raise type /
  opponent can afford to fold / I won't become pot-committed / I can bet enough to make them uncomfortable.
