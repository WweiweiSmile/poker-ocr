**Continuation-bet frequency must be taken per heads-up scenario, never a blanket frequency**:

| Scenario | Bet frequency | Sizing |
|---|---|---|
| I raised preflop, opponent called in position | 65% (15-20% of which is pure bluff with nothing) | about 1/2 pot |
| I raised preflop, out-of-position opponent called then checked | 85% (drop to 65% vs a check-raise type) | about 1/2 pot |
| Opponent raised, I called in position, they check to me | 50% | — |
| Small blind limped, I checked the big blind, they check to me | 75-80% | — |
| Someone limped, small blind folded, I checked the big blind | 65% | — |

**Four-factor sizing method** (four answers don't get averaged):
1. How strong is my hand against their range: very strong → 1/3 pot; medium → 2/3 pot; weak but want to bet → full pot
2. How likely my hand is to improve: unlikely → more than 2/3 pot; 15-20% → 2/3 pot; over 34% → 1/2 pot
3. How likely the opponent has a pair or better: unlikely → 1/3 pot; looks like a pair and I'm best → 2/3 pot;
   looks like two pair or better and I'm best → full pot; I'm not best → almost never bet
4. How likely the opponent has a premium draw (8+ outs): likely and I'm best → full pot;
   likely and I'm not best → almost never bet

**Bet first on paired boards**: on flops like 664, 992, T33, K66, KK6, betting first usually takes the pot;
sizing 1/3-1/2 pot. When you hit, use the exact same amount so the opponent can't read it.
