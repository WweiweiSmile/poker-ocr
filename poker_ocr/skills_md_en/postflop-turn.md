> Source: "Little Green Book" methodology §8, key turn numbers (body unmodified).

- **"The turn is no place for fancy play."** Bet when you have the best hand; almost never give a free river card.
- When a scare card (A, K, or a card that completes a flush/straight) hits, yield the betting lead and check-call to control the pot —
  a free river card only hurts you about 20% of the time. **Exception**: when the opponent already showed weakness on the flop, apply pressure to win the pot instead.

- When the turn makes you a full house while the opponent is on a flush/straight draw, **bet 1/3 pot** — give them odds that look right,
  when in fact they're already drawing dead.
