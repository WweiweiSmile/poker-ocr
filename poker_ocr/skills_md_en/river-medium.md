> Source: "Little Green Book" methodology §9, key river numbers (body unmodified).

- **Betting a medium-strength hand out of position on the river is one of the worst errors** (it's only called by better and only folds out worse,
  and it loses the chance to induce a bluff). With a medium hand, check to induce a bluff, or check-call.

- A good player won't bet a medium hand on the river. If they bet a blank board, lean toward reading it as a bluff.
