> Source: "Little Green Book" methodology §7, key flop numbers (body unmodified).

By made-hand type:
- **Two pair**: only improves 17% of the time — plan to win with two pair. Top two pair ≈ 85% equity,
  bottom two 76%, **top-and-bottom two pair is the most fragile at only 73%** (opponent has 6 outs).
- **A set**: the goal is to make the opponent make their biggest mistake. Out of position with the opponent looking like top pair/overpair → bet to get raised;
  in position with the opponent looking weak → check and let them bet. Flop with straight or flush potential → bet about 3/4 pot.
  Set over set is about 1/100; don't change your play for it.
- **Trips (board paired)**: opponent looks like an overpair → bet to get raised; opponent looks like a middle pair → slow-play.
- **Straight**: a 0- or 1-gap top (nut) straight, no flush possible → 1/2 pot, with flush possible → 2/3, three to a flush → full pot;
  a 0- or 1-gap bottom straight almost always → full pot or overbet; a 2-gap straight with flush possible → 2/3, no flush → 1/2.
- **Flush**: nut flush slow-play (another suited card on the turn, ~17%, kills all action);
  opponent looks like top pair/overpair → full pot; non-nut flush → 1/2 pot.
- **Full house**: 55 on an AA5 board has only 77% equity — play it aggressively;
  AA on an A55 board is the only full house you can slow-play.
