> Source: "Little Green Book" methodology §6, key preflop numbers (split from the original `coachMethodology`, body unmodified; the "Appendix" at the end is this project's addition, not the original).

- Open-raise sizing: early position 2.5-3.0× / middle 3.0-3.5 / late 3.5-4.0 / small blind 3.0×.
  **Never size by hand strength** — don't let the opponent read your hand.

- **When the size stops working, go bigger**: if 3× BB gets no respect and many call, tighten the range and raise to 5-10 BB.

- Always raise as the first to enter. Limping is allowed only in three situations: trapping with a monster / the blinds are weak and you can outplay them postflop /
  a reverse-raise bait in position. Marginal and strong hands mixed 4:1 works best.

### Appendix. Open-Raise Sizing Formula (this project's addition, not the original)

The original only gives per-position base multipliers and says nothing about **antes** and **limpers** — both inflate the pot
early; not adjusting is effectively discounting for the players behind. Formula:

**Open to = position base multiplier × (×2 if there's an ante) × 1bb + limper count × 1bb**

(limper count = players who limped in before me; the big blind counts as 1bb)

| Position | Base multiplier | 3 limpers, no ante | 3 limpers, with ante |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early | 2.5-3.0× | 5.5-6bb | 8-9bb |
| Middle | 3.0-3.5× | 6-6.5bb | 9-10bb |
| Late | 3.5-4.0× | 6.5-7bb | **10-11bb** |
| Small blind | 3.0× | 6bb | 9bb |

- **Why ×2 with an ante**: the ante puts dead money in the pot early, as if everyone is seeing a cheap flop.
  If you don't raise the size, limpers are getting a profitable price with any two cards.
- **Why +1bb per limper**: each extra player already in the pot gives the callers behind better odds,
  and adds one more opponent to worry about postflop. Adding 1bb makes "one more player in" always cost money.

**Always express sizing in BB multiples or a BB count, never "raise to N× the pot"** —
antes and limpers inflate the pot, so pot-multiple sizing is undefined; the same size computed under different conventions can differ by 2×.

**Relationship to "when the size stops working, go bigger" above**: that one is about post-hoc adjustment after your open has already been called by a bunch of players;
this formula governs the pre-open size when there are already antes or limpers. Use the formula before the open, and only talk about going bigger afterward —
don't treat that rule's "5-10 BB" as a ceiling that overrides this formula (late position with ante + 3 limpers already reaches 10-11bb).
